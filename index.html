Before you order

A few useful
little answers.

Because handmade orders work a little differently from adding something to a big-box cart.

How do I place an order?

Add the pieces you want to your basket and tap “Send order on WhatsApp”. The message includes each product's ID, name, quantity and total so we can confirm everything together.

Can I change the colour or size?

For many pieces, yes. Custom colours, sizes and small personal touches can be discussed on WhatsApp before the order is confirmed.

How long does a handmade order take?

It depends on the item and current queue. Ready pieces can move faster; made-to-order and custom pieces take longer. The exact timeline is confirmed before payment.

How does payment work?

Payment details are confirmed directly on WhatsApp after your order is reviewed. Do not send payment until the final amount and payment method have been confirmed.

Are the photos exact?

These are temporary stock/demo photos for the website. They will be replaced with Divyanshi's own product photographs before launch so customers see the actual pieces they are ordering.
